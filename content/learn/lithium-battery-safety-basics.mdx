## Charge safely

Use the charger that came with the battery, charge on a hard surface and do not leave it charging unattended overnight.

## Store it right

Keep batteries at room temperature, away from heat and moisture, and partially charged for long storage.

## Know the warning signs

Swelling, a hot battery when idle, a burning smell or hissing mean stop using it and move it outside if safe to do so.
