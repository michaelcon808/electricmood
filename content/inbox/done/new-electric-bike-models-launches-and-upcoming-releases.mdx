# New Electric Bikes 2027: Launches and Models

The biggest new electric bike 2027 news so far is on the parts side. Bosch announced its first hub motor in June 2026 as part of its 2027 model year, and Cube's 2027 range, launched August 12, 2026, already includes a bike built around it. In the US, the launch most shoppers will meet is the Lectric XPress2 at $1,399 (launched May 19, 2026), and Velotric spent 2026 refreshing its whole lineup. Most of the rest is announced rather than on sale, and I say which is which.

That split matters for any new electric bike 2027 shopper. A launch article tells you what a maker says. It rarely tells you how the bike rides, how far it really goes or whether it holds up. I haven't ridden any of these bikes, so everything here is a maker claim, a trade-show announcement or a published test, and I label each one. Where a bike has already been tested by an independent site, I give the result.

My sources are the makers' own announcements as reported by Electrek, electrive, Cube's 2027 press release, Eurobike's 2026 press handout, Velotric's site, and the testers I use in my [best electric bikes](/best-electric-bikes-and-ebikes-for-adults-reviews/) guide. ## New electric bike releases 2027: US launches

### Lectric XPress2: the budget launch of 2026

Lectric's XPress2 is the most concrete US launch I found. Electrek reported it as officially launched on May 19, 2026, at $1,399, in two versions: a high-step Commuter and a step-through Cruiser. Everything below is Lectric's claim, relayed by Electrek, and none of it is independently tested yet.

- **Motor:** Stealth M24 rear hub, 750 W nominal, 1,310 W peak, 85 Nm.
- **Battery:** removable 48V 14Ah, 672 Wh, with a claimed range of up to 60 miles.
- **Speed and class:** adjustable between classes 1, 2 and 3, up to 28 mph.
- **Weight:** 63 lb, or 55 lb without the battery.
- **Ride parts:** torque-sensing assist with a cadence option, 180 mm hydraulic brakes, an 80 mm suspension fork, a Shimano Altus 8-speed and a 3.5 inch color display.
- **Certification:** Lectric says the battery is UL 2271 and the bike UL 2849.

Lectric also says the motor is "99% quieter" than before, which I can't verify. The article doesn't give ship dates or the original XPress's specs, so I can't tell you how big the upgrade is. I'd wait for an independent range test before treating 60 miles as real, since the 60-mile figure is a claim and the testers I trust usually land well under or well over claims depending on assist level. For how those tests work, see my [longest range e-bike guide](/longest-range-electric-bikes/).

### Segway Myon and Muxi: announced at CES, one now tested

Segway's January 6, 2026 CES release named two e-bikes, the Myon for commuting and leisure with adaptive electronic shifting, and the Muxi, a "versatile lifestyle" bike with traction control, regenerative braking and a smart control panel. It said both were "coming to the North American market this year" and gave no specs or prices. The Myon has since been tested: OutdoorGearLab (updated July 9, 2026) lists it at $1,999, with a 722 Wh battery, a measured 73.1 lb, a 36.1 mile range in its throttle-only test, a score of 84 and the label best for steep hills. I found no price, spec or independent test for the Muxi, so treat it as announced only.

### Things sold as e-bikes that may not be

Electrek's May 2026 coverage included a Heybike Saturn at $999 with a 40 mph headline claim (Electrek's headline calls it an electric moped), a Juiced electric dirt bike and a Yozma IN10 Pro. I didn't verify any of those specs. The point is the class: Wikipedia's [summary of e-bike laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_bicycle_laws) says a federal low-speed electric bicycle has working pedals, a motor under 750 W and a motor-only top speed under 20 mph. A bike that goes 40 mph on its motor is outside that, so local rules on where you can ride it may be very different. See my guide to [e-bike classes](/what-are-the-different-e-bike-classes-class-1-2-3-and-4/).

### Cube's 2027 range (launched August 12, 2026)

Cube's 2027 press release gives August 12, 2026 as the launch date for its new bikes and accessories. The e-bikes named are:

- **Editor Hybrid 360 FE (new):** a Bosch hub-drive commuter with a 360 Wh battery, which makes it one of the first bikes built around Bosch's new motor.
- **Nuride Hybrid ONE11 (new):** a fully equipped tourer with a Bosch CX motor, an 800 Wh battery and 100 mm of suspension front and rear.

### Riese & Müller Veya and Multicharger4

At its Campus Days event on June 24 to 26, 2026, Riese & Müller presented its 2027 catalog, according to Interempresas (July 7, 2026). Bosch is a major supplier of e-bike motors, and several bikes in this guide use its parts, so its announcements shape many 2027 electric bike models. The news was reported between June 20 and 26, 2026, from an event in Hamburg, and the retailer E-Bikeshop wrote it up on June 25.

|---|---|---|
| Hub Line motor | Bosch's first hub motor, 2.3 kg, 45 Nm, about 10 cm across, assistance stops above 25 km/h (15.5 mph) | Part of the 2027 model year. Power not stated by Bosch; a UK retailer lists 400 W peak |

| "Certified by Bosch" | A digital certificate for used e-bikes; Rebike first, from July 2026 | Retailers from early 2027 |

What this means in practice:

- **A new electric bike 2027 with a Hub Line motor** will be a light city bike. A 45 Nm hub is not a hill climber: the picks in my [best electric bikes](/best-electric-bikes-and-ebikes-for-adults-reviews/) guide run from 55 Nm (Lectric XP4) to 100 Nm (Aventon Level 4 ADV).
- **The 360 Wh battery is small.** Compare the Velotric Tempo, which has 374 Wh and was tested at 71 miles in Eco. Bosch's "over 50 miles" is a claim, and the Tempo shows what a small battery can do when you pedal.
- **Retailer-reported specs are not Bosch's.** The 400 W peak, the charger timing and "free" software come from a UK shop's blog, so I would check them against Bosch before relying on them.

No date or price for bikes using the Hub Line was given by Bosch, and no US availability. A reader asked electrive whether the PowerTube 360 would be sold worldwide and the article doesn't say.

## Electric bike new model 2027: how to judge a launch

A new electric bike 2027 model is not automatically better than last year's. Here is the checklist I use, built from what these launches did and did not tell me.

1. **Is it on sale?** For any electric bike new model 2027 shoppers see, look for a date. Cube gave one. Segway's Muxi and Bosch's Hub Line bikes did not.
2. **Is the number tested or claimed?** Lectric's 60 mile range, Velotric's 95 miles for the Discover M and Bosch's "over 50 miles" are claims. Velotric's own Discover 3 claim was 80 miles and Electric Bike Review tested 87 in Eco and 38 in Turbo, so a claim can be low or high depending on assist level.
3. **What is the class and the law?** Lectric says the XPress2 can be set to class 1, 2 or 3 up to 28 mph. Many places treat class 3 differently, so read my [e-bike laws guide](/pa-california-florida-new-jersey-electric-bike-laws/) before you unlock it.
4. **Is the battery certified?** Lectric says the XPress2 battery is UL 2271 and the bike UL 2849. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission counted at least 208 micromobility fire or overheating incidents in 39 states between January 2021 and November 2022, with at least 19 deaths, in a December 2022 [CPSC news release](https://cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2023/CPSC-Calls-on-Manufacturers-to-Comply-with-Safety-Standards-for-Battery-Powered-Products-to-Reduce-the-Risk-of-Injury-and-Death), and asked manufacturers to certify to UL standards. I couldn't verify certification for the other bikes here.
5. **Does an independent site test it?** Of the bikes here, the Myon, Discover 3, Tempo and GoMad have tests. The XPress2, Discover M, Summit 2 and every 2027 European bike don't, as far as I found.
6. **What is the price today?** Velotric's prices differ between sources, and sales are running now.

If a bike fails the first two checks, wait. If it passes, compare it to the established picks in my [best electric bikes](/best-electric-bikes-and-ebikes-for-adults-reviews/) guide.
