# Best Electric Skateboards 2027: Top Picks for Every Rider

The best electric skateboard for most riders who want one board to do everything is the Meepo Voyager X: about 30 miles of GPS-verified range, a 34 mph top speed and a sealed gear drive with no belts to replace, for under $1,000. If you want a first board, the Meepo V5 is the easy, forgiving choice, and if you want comfort and polish for daily commuting, look at the OMW Lancer.

Those are my picks for the best electric skateboards 2027 has to offer so far, but a skateboard is a more personal purchase than most e-rides. Deck length, drivetrain, wheel size and battery voltage change how a board feels, and the right one depends on where you ride and how heavy a board you're willing to carry. This guide gives you 12 boards, the numbers behind each one, and a short guide to choosing.

I'll be straight about how this was built. I haven't ridden these boards and I won't claim I have. My picks are based on ERideHero's July 2026 buying guide, which gives real-world range and the maker's claims side by side, and I paraphrase its findings and say where a number is a claim and where it's a test result. I couldn't find a second independent e-skateboard test I trust as much, so treat this as one tester's view and read a hands-on review of any board you're serious about. I'm writing in October 2026 and I'll update the guide as 2027 boards launch.

## How I chose these boards

You'll find plenty of best electric skateboard reviews 2027 shoppers can read, and many just repeat a spec sheet. Here's the method behind mine.

1. **Tested range over claimed range.** Claimed range is the most inconsistent number in this market, because brands measure it in different lab setups. ERideHero's rule of thumb is to divide a battery's watt-hours by 20 to get a realistic range in miles at a normal commuting pace. A 216 Wh board comes out near 10 miles, which matches what testers saw on the WowGo 2S Max.
2. **Battery size and voltage.** Watt-hours tell you how much energy a board stores, and voltage affects how well it holds power as the battery drains.
3. **Ride quality.** Deck flex, trucks, wheels and ESC tuning decide whether a board feels smooth or harsh.
4. **Build and drivetrain.** Hub, belt and gear drives each suit different budgets, and I explain the trade-offs below.
5. **Weight.** A board you can't carry up the stairs is a different purchase from one you can.
6. **Brand and support.** All of these boards come from direct-to-consumer brands, where only the maker sells replacement parts. ERideHero's testers weigh the community's experience of after-sales support, and I've included that where they did.

I left out Onewheel-style boards, which are a different product. If you're curious about them, see my page on [Onewheel reviews and prices](/onewheel-electric-skateboard-reviews-price/).

## Best electric skateboards under $500

Under $500 gets you a short-range commuter board that's good for campus or the last mile. Don't expect all-day range at this price.

### WowGo 2S Max: best budget longboard

ERideHero calls it arguably the most reliable board you can buy under $400, with a strong long-term ownership record across the community. It has dual 550 W hub motors, a 216 Wh 12S battery (a higher voltage class than most boards at this price), a Hobbywing ESC known for smooth acceleration and braking, and a real 38-inch flexy maple-and-bamboo deck.

The catch is range. The claimed figure is 14.3 miles, but tested range is closer to 10, so it's a short-commute and campus board. It also reaches its claimed 28 mph for average-weight riders. You can pick softer 105 mm honeycomb wheels at checkout for rough roads, at the cost of about 10 percent of range. If you want a longer list at this price, read my guide to [budget electric skateboards](/best-budget-electric-skateboards-under-1000/).

### Meepo V5 (144 Wh): best first board

The V5 is Meepo's fifth-generation entry board. It weighs 17.6 pounds, the lightest full-size longboard in the guide, with a cutout handle that makes it easy to carry, and it has dual 500 W hub motors, a 38.1-inch maple deck and a 330 lb load rating. ## Best mid-range electric skateboards

Mid-range boards sit between the budget tier and the performance tier, and they're where most commuters should look. ERideHero describes the Voyager X and the Pioneer X5 as under $1,000. You get 12S to 14S batteries, roughly 19 to 30 real miles of range and a much better ride.

### Acedeck Stella Blizzard (belt): best specs for the money

ERideHero calls this the most hardware per dollar. It has a 518 Wh 14S2P Samsung battery, dual 3,000 W belt motors, CNC-machined trucks and motor mounts, and a 100 A FOC ESC with a smooth throttle. Real-world range is about 23 miles, and a 14S system holds its power and climbs hills even near the end of a charge.

The downsides are a stiff deck with hard 85 mm wheels, so it suits smooth pavement (you can upgrade the wheels), pricey spare parts and only a 6-month warranty on the electronics.

### Tynee Mini 3 Pro (belt): most powerful shortboard

A 29.6-inch kicktail board with dual 2,775 W belt motors that deliver 32 mph out of the box and can be unlocked to 37 mph from the remote. The 2026 version has a 468 Wh pack, about 19 percent more battery than before, and ERideHero measured roughly 19 miles of range. Tynee also has a reputation for the best support in the industry, with real replies within hours, according to ERideHero's read of the community.

Beginners will likely find it overwhelming, though the ESC can be tuned down. Belts wear fast with this much torque, so expect about 300 miles per set, and the warranty is 6 months.

### OMW Lancer: best mid-range ride quality

This is the most comfortable board in its price range. Its adjustable wheelbase offers three flex settings, and it comes with forged TKP trucks, 105 mm Mad Wheelz V2 wheels (the best stock wheels at the price, per ERideHero), a 504 Wh 14S2P pack, dual 1,457 W belt motors and a carbon composite deck. It has an IP55 rating, a 12-month warranty including electronics and a lifetime deck guarantee. Range is about 22 miles at 31 mph.

It isn't a torque monster next to the Blizzard or the Mini 3 Pro, and at nearly 40 inches it has a big footprint. OMW started in 2023, so its track record is short but good so far.

## Best performance electric skateboards

### Acedeck Nomad N1 (belt): best performance for the money

The N1 has a battery of up to 1,036 Wh (14S4P), dual 3,500 W belt motors and a rated top speed of 40 mph, and ERideHero measured about 33 miles of range. The split-angle deck, borrowed from downhill racing, makes it stable at speed, and the trucks, mounts and hubs are CNC-machined. It comes in belt, gear and all-terrain versions.

It's heavy at 36 pounds, so it's a board you ride from your door, not one you carry. An early batch had ESC problems that have been fixed, so buy current stock.

You pay a premium for the build. Real range is about 23 miles from a 504 Wh pack, top speed is 31 mph and it handles 20 percent hills. The hubs are proprietary, which limits wheel swaps.

## How to choose an electric skateboard

These are the specs I'd weigh, in order of importance.

1. **Watt-hours, not claimed range.** Watt-hours tell you how much energy the battery holds. Divide by 20 for a realistic range in miles at a normal pace.
2. **Battery voltage.** Higher voltage delivers power more strongly and holds up as the battery drains. ERideHero says 14S boards stay punchy at low charge while 10S boards usually fade below half, and that 12S is the minimum if you plan to ride steep hills.
3. **Drivetrain.** Hub motors are cheap, quiet and maintenance-free but ride harsher. Belts add torque and speed and let you swap wheels, but they wear out every few hundred miles. Gear drives are sealed and long-lasting but cost more.
4. **Weight.** Under 20 pounds is easy to carry, and above about 30 pounds gets awkward. If you're taking stairs or public transport, weight matters a lot.
5. **Deck and wheels.** A stiff deck with hard wheels is harsh on rough roads. Bigger, softer wheels help.
6. **Brand and support.** ERideHero's testers say Tynee, Backfire and WowGo have strong reputations for standing behind their boards, while Meepo, Acedeck and the younger brands vary more.
7. **Water resistance.** Ratings here range from IPX5 to IP65. If you ride in wet weather, read my guide on [whether you can skateboard in the rain](/can-you-skateboard-in-the-rain-electric-skateboard/).
8. **Use case.** If you'll mostly commute or ride around campus, see my guide to [electric skateboards for college campus commuting](/best-electric-skateboard-for-college-campus-commuting/).

## Can you fly with these boards?

This is a point most roundups miss. Aviation rules for lithium batteries are based on watt-hours. As I explain in my guide on [bringing a skateboard on a plane](/can-you-bring-or-take-a-skateboard-on-a-plane-or-airplane/), the [FAA's PackSafe guidance](https://www.faa.gov/hazmat/packsafe/lithium-batteries) limits lithium-ion batteries to 100 Wh each, with up to two spares of 101 to 160 Wh allowed with airline approval, and the FAA's [packing poster](https://www.faa.gov/hazmat/packsafe/resources/spare-lithium-batteries-packing-poster-landscape.pdf) lists 161 Wh and above as forbidden.

Compare that with the batteries in this guide. The Meepo V5 (144 Wh) and the Maxfind Cyber Mini (158.4 Wh) fall in the 101 to 160 Wh range, so they'd need airline approval. Every other board here has a battery above 160 Wh, which under those limits can't travel as a passenger item. That's a real consideration if you fly often. Airlines can be stricter than the FAA, and I haven't checked any airline's page, so always confirm with yours before you travel.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best electric skateboards 2027?
For most riders, the Meepo Voyager X is the best all-rounder, the Meepo V5 is the best first board, and the OMW Lancer is the most comfortable for commuting.

### What do the best electric skateboard reviews 2027 say about range?
They agree that claimed range is optimistic. ERideHero tested the Voyager X at about 30 miles and the Cyber Max X at about 28, but found the 2S Max closer to 10 than its claimed 14.3.

### Which electric skateboard 2027 is best for beginners?
The Meepo V5, thanks to its gentle low modes and light weight. The WowGo 2S Max is a close second.

### What is the best long range electric skateboard 2027?
The Acedeck Nomad N1 had the longest tested range at about 33 miles, and the Meepo Voyager X offers about 30 miles at a lighter 24 pounds.

### What is the fastest electric skateboard 2027 has to offer here?
The Acedeck Nomad N1 is rated at 40 mph. The Tynee Mini 3 Pro and Nyx Z3 are rated at 37 mph.

### What is the electric skateboard price range and prices 2027 riders should expect?
Under $500 for short-range hub-motor boards, up to about $1,000 for mid-range belt and gear-drive boards, and more for performance and mountainboards. Check current prices before buying.

### Are belt or gear drive skateboards better?
Belts give more torque and let you swap wheels but need replacing every few hundred miles. Gear drives are sealed and last longer but cost more.

### How far can an electric skateboard go?
Tested range in this guide runs from about 8 miles on the smallest boards to about 33 miles on the Nomad N1.

### How heavy are these boards?
From 16 pounds for the Cyber Mini to 44.5 pounds for the Nyx Z3. Under 20 pounds is easy to carry.

### Can I ride an electric skateboard in the rain?
Some are rated IPX5 to IP65, but light rain is the limit. My rain guide covers it.

### Can I take an electric skateboard on a plane?
Only the two smallest batteries here fall within the 160 Wh limit, and they need airline approval. See the section above.

### Are electric skateboards safe for beginners?
They can be, with a forgiving board, low speed modes, a helmet and a quiet place to practice.
