# Best Electric Skateboard Brands 2027

The best electric skateboard brands 2027 start with Backfire, Meepo and WowGo, the names that appear most across the tests and rankings I read, plus Acedeck, Tynee and OMW for newer boards and Exway and Evolve for established ranges. If you want something unlike a skateboard, Onewheel is the best-known brand but carries a safety history you should read first. Whichever brand you choose, the warranty is short: six months is the figure sources give for most of them.

This best electric skateboard brands ranking is not a sales ranking, because no source I found publishes sales by brand. It is a read of what named reviewers recommend, how long each brand has been around, what they say about support and what happened to Boosted, the best-known brand that disappeared. I show where the sources disagree.

I haven't ridden any of these boards. The page draws on ERideHero (updated July 21, 2026), TechGearLab, ReviewFinder (a meta-ranking of other publishers' lists), Concrete Waves, Wikipedia, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission and the FAA. I label what is tested and what is a claim. Prices are what the named sources listed, not live prices. Checked October 10, 2026.

## How I chose these brands and what the sources measure

Each source measures something different, so here is what they cover and where they fall short.

- **ERideHero** (updated July 21, 2026, by Rasmus Barslund): says it has tested 120+ products across 12,000+ miles since 2019. Some range figures are measured (Cyber Max X 28 mi, Voyager X 30 mi GPS-verified, Pioneer X5 22 mi, Nomad N1 about 33 mi, Nyx Z3 just shy of 26 mi, Zealot X2 23 mi) and others are estimates with no stated method. Its brand support remarks are opinions.
- **TechGearLab:** scores 30 boards from 21 brands and says it buys all products and accepts no freebies, with "hands-on and lab testing" and no further detail. The page gives no update date, and several boards it scores, such as the Boosted models, are no longer made.
- **ReviewFinder** (January 9, 2025): an aggregate. It collected top-10 lists from 10 publishers updated within nine months, counted 83 endorsements of 68 boards and ranked 13. It is a count of recommendations, not a test.
- **Concrete Waves** (published May 4, 2023, modified April 14, 2024): lists eight brands with headquarters and warranty terms, no ranking method and no testing. Its warranty figures are older and may have changed.
- **Wikipedia, the CPSC and the FAA** for brand history, safety and flight rules.

None of these is a long-term reliability study. I found no brand-level failure-rate survey, and Trustpilot-style review pages don't tell you much about a young brand's service. So treat "best" as "recommended by named reviewers," not "least likely to break."

## Onewheel: the best-known brand, and the safety record

TechGearLab gives the Onewheel GT its Top Pick (4.8, $2,300), and Concrete Waves' author, who owns a Onewheel, calls it "super fun for trail riding." It is a single-wheel board, not a traditional skateboard, which Concrete Waves also notes.

The safety record matters. In November 2022 the CPSC [warned consumers to stop using all Onewheel models](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2023/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Stop-Using-Onewheel-Self-Balancing-Electric-Skateboards-Due-to-Ejection-Hazard-At-Least-Four-Deaths-and-Multiple-Injuries-Reported), citing at least four deaths and multiple injuries, and in September 2023 Future Motion announced a [recall of about 300,000 boards](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2023/Future-Motion-Recalls-Onewheel-Self-Balancing-Electric-Skateboards-Due-to-Crash-Hazard-Four-Deaths-Reported) with a firmware remedy. I cover the details, the current models and what I could and couldn't verify in my [Onewheel reviews and prices](/onewheel-electric-skateboard-reviews-price/) guide. Concrete Waves lists Onewheel's warranty as six months to a year, while the company's own site, as I read it for that guide, states 12 months, so check at purchase. ## When a brand disappears: Boosted

Boosted is the cautionary tale. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boosted_(company)), the Mountain View, California company was founded in 2012, raised over $60 million by December 2018 and made some of the boards TechGearLab still scores at 4.8. Its Rev scooter was hit by the U.S.-China tariff dispute. On March 4, 2020, Boosted announced layoffs and said it was seeking a buyer, and it was defunct by 2020. Lime bought some assets, including five core patents, in April 2020, and Boosted USA bought the remaining inventory. Wikipedia's summary doesn't say what happened to warranties or support for owners. I didn't find what became of them.

The lesson: a high score on a discontinued board is not a buying recommendation. Before you buy, check whether the brand still sells and supports the board, and whether spare batteries and parts are available. This is my own advice, based on the Boosted case.

## How to judge an electric skateboard brand yourself

1. **Warranty.** The documented terms are short: six months is common (Backfire, Exway, Tynee, Acedeck's electronics, Meepo Voyager X), Evolve is one year, and OMW is 12 months with a lifetime deck guarantee. Read the terms for battery and electronics separately.
2. **Support reputation.** ERideHero's remarks (Tynee strong, WowGo strong, Acedeck fair but slow, Meepo hit-or-miss) are one reviewer's views. Look for owner reports before buying.
3. **Battery size and flying.** The [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/hazmat/packsafe/lithium-batteries) allows spare lithium batteries up to 100 Wh in carry-on without approval, 101 to 160 Wh with airline approval, and none above 160 Wh. ERideHero's July 2026 guide gives a rough rule: divide watt-hours by 20 for commuting range, which is the reviewer's estimate. Wikipedia describes mini boards with detachable packs under 99 Wh as air travel safe. My [plane guide](/can-you-bring-or-take-a-skateboard-on-a-plane-or-airplane/) goes through the cases.
4. **Drivetrain.** Wikipedia lists hub, direct, belt and gear drives. ERideHero's Acedeck and Tynee picks are belt-drive boards. Belt drives are often recommended for hills; Concrete Waves says so without evidence.
5. **Water.** ERideHero praises the Maxfind boards for IPX5 water resistance; see [riding in the rain](/can-you-skateboard-in-the-rain-electric-skateboard/).
6. **Helmet.** Wikipedia cites a 2018 to 2022 study, published in December 2024 in the American Journal of Emergency Medicine, that found more hospitalizations and multiple fractures per accident for electric skateboards than for traditional ones. Wear a helmet and protective gear; see [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_skateboard). That is not brand advice, but it matters more than any brand.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best electric skateboard brands 2027?
Backfire, Meepo and WowGo appear most across the tests and rankings I read, with Acedeck, Tynee and OMW for newer boards and Exway and Evolve for established ranges.

### What is the best electric skateboard brands ranking 2027?
There is no sales ranking. ReviewFinder's meta-ranking counts reviewer recommendations: Backfire G2 first, then the Exway Flex Pro and Backfire Zealot S2.

### Is Onewheel safe?
The CPSC warned in November 2022 and Future Motion recalled about 300,000 boards in 2023 with a firmware fix. See my Onewheel guide for details.
