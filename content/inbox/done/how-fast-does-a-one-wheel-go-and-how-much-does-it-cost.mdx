# Onewheel Speed and Cost: How Fast and How Much? 2027

How fast does a one wheel go? It depends on the model: Wikipedia lists top speeds from 13 mph on the original 2014 board to 25 mph on the GT S-Series, with most current boards at 16 to 20 mph. And how much are one wheels? Third-party price pages I checked put new boards at roughly $950 to $3,500, depending on the model.

Both numbers come with a safety caveat that I think belongs in this guide, not at the bottom: in 2023 the US Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall of 300,000 Onewheel boards after four deaths, because a board can stop balancing its rider when its limits are exceeded. So top speed on a Onewheel is not just a spec; it is close to a limit the board warns you about.

I haven't ridden or tested a Onewheel. Speeds come from Wikipedia's model table, prices from third-party price pages and the recall notice from the CPSC, and I say where sources disagree.

## How fast does a one wheel go? Top speed by model

Here are the models listed by [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onewheel), with the speed and range figures it gives. These are maker specifications as reported there, not independent tests; I found no independent speed tests to quote.

| Model | Launched | Top speed | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onewheel (original) | January 2014 | 13 mph | 4 to 6 mi |
| Onewheel+ | January 2017 | 19 mph | 5 to 7 mi |
| Onewheel+ XR | 2018 | 19 mph | 12 to 18 mi |
| Pint | 2019 | 16 mph | 6 to 8 mi |
| Pint X | October 2021 | 18 mph | 12 to 18 mi |
| GT | October 2021 | 20 mph | 20 to 32 mi |
| GT S-Series | October 2023 | 25 mph | 16 to 25 mi |
| Pint S | September 2024 | 20 mph | 12 to 18 mi |
| XR Classic | November 2024 | 20 mph | 17 to 24 mi |

The Rally XL, launched in August 2025 per Wikipedia, has no speed or range listed there. A launch report on the GT and Pint X from October 2021 agrees with the table: GT 20 mph and 32 mile maximum range, Pint X 18 mph. So how fast does a one wheel go in daily use? Up to the figure for your model, but see the safety section below before treating it as a target.

**Range moves with speed.** The same launch report quotes the GT at 20 to 32 miles "depending on speed, weight, and other factors", which is the usual pattern: riding faster uses more battery.

Page A lists these add-ons and running costs. They are that page's figures, not mine:

- Pint fender: $75, and rail guards: $25
- Helmet (Triple 8): $85
- Wrist guards (Full Sender): $95
- Battery replacement: $300 to $600

Page A also shows a Pint X starter setup at $1,480, or $1,590 with the Ultracharger, before checkout charges. Think of a realistic first-year cost as the board plus protective gear, not the board alone.

### Used Onewheels

Page A gives only two asking-price snapshots: a used Pint X at $850, and a used original XR at $1,450 with upgrades and a disclosed operating issue. Asking prices are not sales, and two listings are not a market. If you shop used, note that page A lists recall remedy status, battery health and tire condition among the things that drive price.

## The safety question behind Onewheel speed

The CPSC's recall notice, dated September 29, 2023, covers 300,000 boards: the original Onewheel, Onewheel+, +XR, Pint, Pint X and GT. In its words, the boards "can stop balancing the rider when their limits are exceeded, posing a crash hazard that can result in serious injury or death." It reports dozens of incidents, including four deaths between 2019 and 2021 from head trauma, with at least three of the riders not wearing helmets. Reported injuries include traumatic brain injury, paralysis, fractures and ligament damage. The CPSC told owners to stop using the boards immediately. The full notice is on the [CPSC site](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2023/Future-Motion-Recalls-Onewheel-Self-Balancing-Electric-Skateboards-Due-to-Crash-Hazard-Four-Deaths-Reported).

The remedies it describes:

- **GT, Pint X, Pint and XR:** a firmware update through the Onewheel app adding "Haptic Buzz", a vibration and sound warning near the board's limits or in low battery or error states.
- **Original Onewheel and Onewheel+:** a pro-rated store credit after confirming disposal.

Earlier, in August 2022, a voluntary recall covered about 20,500 GT front footpads after 813 reports of the footpad failing to disengage after dismount, per Wikipedia, which also says Future Motion initially objected to the CPSC's November 2022 warning and said the boards are safe when ridden with standard safety practices, including helmets.

Wikipedia lists the board's built-in safety features: a pressure-sensitive footpad that detects whether a rider is on the board, optional Simplestop (from the Pint onward) that slows and disengages the board when the rider leans back, Pushback that gently raises the nose near unsafe speeds, and Haptic Buzz.

**What I take from this.** For a Onewheel, a spec of 20 mph is not the same as a skateboard's 20 mph: the board balances for you until it can't, and the warnings arrive near the maximum. I'd choose a model for the range and weight I need and ride well below the limit, wear a helmet and wrist protection every time, and check the recall status of any used board before buying. This is my opinion, not medical or safety advice.

## How a Onewheel works and how it differs from a skateboard

Wikipedia describes the Onewheel as self-balancing: you lean forward to accelerate and back to slow down, and accelerometers and gyroscopes take readings about 14,000 times a second so the motor can keep you upright. The CPSC calls it a self-balancing electric skateboard, but it has one wheel and you control it by leaning, which is why speed and safety work differently from a four-wheel board. For a standard four-wheel board, see [what is an e-board](/what-is-an-e-board-electric-skateboard/) and [how fast do electric skateboards go](/how-fast-do-electric-skateboards-go/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How fast does a one wheel go at the top end?
The GT S-Series is listed at 25 mph on Wikipedia, the highest in its model table.

### How fast does a one wheel go for beginners?
The Pint is listed at 16 mph and the Pint X at 18 mph. I haven't found a source that gives a recommended beginner speed, so ride well under the top figure.

### How fast does a one wheel go compared with an electric skateboard?
Most Onewheels are listed at 16 to 20 mph, with the GT S-Series at 25 mph. Typical four-wheel electric skateboards are quoted at 20 to 25 mph.

### How much are one wheels at the cheapest end?
The Pint is the cheapest current model, at $750 to $950 on the two price pages I checked.

### How much are one wheels in total with gear?
Page A's Pint X starter setup is $1,480, or $1,590 with a charger, before checkout charges. That is one page's example.

### How much are one wheels used?
Page A shows two asking prices, $850 and $1,450, which are not a market average.

### How much does a Onewheel battery replacement cost?
Page A lists $300 to $600.

### Are Onewheels safe?
The CPSC recalled all models in September 2023 after four deaths. Read the safety section above, wear a helmet, and apply the firmware update if your board is eligible.

### Do old Onewheels get the firmware fix?
No. The CPSC says the original Onewheel and Onewheel+ are not eligible and are offered a store credit.

## The bottom line

So, how fast does a one wheel go? About 16 to 20 mph for most current models and up to 25 mph on the GT S-Series, according to Wikipedia's table. And how much are one wheels? Roughly $950 to $3,500 new on the price pages I checked, plus gear and running costs, with used boards varying widely.

The more useful question may be how fast you should go. The CPSC recall tells you the limit is real.
