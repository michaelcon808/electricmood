## Ride quality

Solid tyres mean zero punctures but more vibration. On smooth bike lanes it's comfortable; on cobbles, less so.

### Braking

A front drum and rear electronic brake combine for predictable stops in the dry.

## Range in the real world

Manufacturers quote range for a light rider on flat ground at low speed. Expect roughly **60–70%** of the claim in daily use.

| Condition | Expected range |
| --- | --- |
| Flat, eco mode, 70 kg rider | ~20 km |
| Hilly, sport mode, 90 kg rider | ~13 km |
