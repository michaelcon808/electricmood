# How to Turn On an E-Bike

How to turn on an electric bike, in the order most guides give it: charge the battery, seat it firmly and turn the key or switch if it has one, press the battery's power button if it has one, then press and hold the power button on the handlebar display until it lights up, and choose an assist level. That is how to turn on a bike with a motor on most models, but the exact buttons differ by maker, so your owner's manual wins over anything here.

I haven't ridden or tested any bike for this page, and I haven't tried these steps myself. They come from support and help pages from Levy, Rad Power Bikes, Upway, Leoguar and Gazelle, which are sellers and makers, so treat them as the makers' and sellers' instructions. I tried to read Bosch's help pages too, but they were down for maintenance, so nothing here is Bosch-specific.

## How to turn on an electric bike, step by step

Levy lays out five steps, and the other pages fill in details:

1. **Charge the battery.** Levy says to use the charger that came with the bike. If you need help, see [how to charge an e-bike](/how-to-charge-an-electric-bike-battery-time-cost-and-how-often/).
2. **Seat the battery and unlock it if needed.** Levy says to turn the key in the battery lock, and that some models use a button or switch instead. Rad's support page describes a key port with Locked and Unlocked positions, and says to reinstall a removed battery until it clicks. Leoguar says many batteries have a rocker switch, push button or key, and to make sure it is in the "On" or "I" position.
3. **Press the battery button, if there is one.** On one Rad model's integrated battery, pressing the button on top shows charge on an LED: solid green is full, blinking green is 60 to 80%, solid red is under 60%, and blinking red is under 10%. That is one model's code; yours may differ.
4. **Hold the power button on the display.** Levy says it usually needs a few seconds, and that a beep or a brightening screen confirms the bike is on. The handlebar display should show battery level, speed and assist mode.
5. **Pick an assist level and start pedaling.** Levy says to choose it on the display or a separate controller.

To turn it off, Upway says to hold the power button for several seconds for a full shutdown rather than a quick press.

## How to start an electric bike moving

How to start an electric bike once it is on depends on the type. On a pedal-assist bike, the motor helps only when you pedal, so turn the cranks. On a throttle bike, the throttle can move the bike without pedaling; see [do e-bikes need pedaling](/how-fast-do-electric-bikes-go-without-pedaling-or-pedals/) for how the two differ and the U.S. speed limits. Start on the lowest assist level in a clear area, and keep your hands on the brake levers while you get used to the pull. My [how to ride an e-bike](/how-to-ride-and-use-an-electric-bike-or-e-bike/) guide covers the first ride, and [what an e-bike is and how it works](/what-is-an-e-bike-how-it-works-and-the-different-e-bike-classes/) explains the parts.

One thing that surprises people: Leoguar says a stuck or pressed brake lever can keep the system disabled, which is the brake cut-off. If the bike is on but the motor won't respond, check that both levers return fully.

## How to turn on an ebike: what differs by model

How to turn on an ebike changes with where the battery sits and what controls it:

- **Removable battery with a key.** Unlock, seat the battery, then use the display button.
- **Integrated battery with its own button.** Press the battery button first, as Rad's page describes for its integrated-battery models, then the display.
- **Display-only power.** Some bikes power everything from the display button once the battery is seated. I didn't find a source describing which makers do this, so check your manual.
- **Anti-theft or lock mode.** Upway says to check the lock or anti-theft mode if the screen is blank, and that unlock procedures vary, so use your manual.
- **App-based units.** Upway mentions updating firmware through a brand's Bluetooth app. I didn't read any steps for turning a bike on from an app.

Lights are often a separate control; your manual will say how they switch on.

## If your e-bike won't turn on: simple checks first

Here are the safe checks the pages agree on, from easiest to hardest:

1. **Charge.** Check the battery has charge and the charger works. Leoguar says a solid green charger light usually means it has power but isn't charging, a solid red light means it's charging, and no light suggests a faulty charger.
2. **Reseat the battery.** Rad and Leoguar both say to remove it, look at the contacts and mounting tray, and reinsert it until it clicks and doesn't wiggle. Rad says that if you see dirt, damage or moisture, to stop and contact support.
3. **Hold the power button longer.** Leoguar says to hold it 2 to 3 seconds and suggests a full five seconds to be safe. Levy says a few seconds. Upway says several seconds for shutdown. The pages don't agree on the exact time, so follow your manual.
4. **Check the display is seated.** Gazelle says a gently inserted display may not make contact with its connectors, and to check that the cables are intact.
5. **Release the brake levers**, as above.
6. **Restart the system.** Gazelle says to turn the motor off on the display, wait 10 seconds, then restart; if that fails, remove the battery, wait briefly, and reinsert it. For a frozen display, Upway suggests holding the power button for about ten seconds, or on some bikes pulling the battery for 30 seconds, and says to confirm the sequence in your manual.
7. **Deeply discharged battery.** Leoguar says the battery management system may be in protection mode and suggests leaving it on the charger 8 to 12 hours. That is one seller's advice; I'd use the supplied charger, stay nearby, and ask the maker if it doesn't recover.
8. **Error codes.** Upway says to check your manual first, since codes differ between motor systems, and to clear a code once; if it returns after restarts, stop clearing it.

If the bike got wet, Upway says to turn it off, remove the battery if you can, stop pressing buttons, and leave it somewhere warm and dry for at least a day before switching it on. See [riding in the rain](/can-you-ride-an-electric-bike-in-the-wet-rain/) and [riding in snow and winter](/can-you-ride-an-ebike-in-the-snow-or-winter/). If the brakes feel off after any of this, see [how to tighten and adjust e-bike brakes](/how-to-tighten-and-adjust-electric-bike-and-ebike-brakes/).

## What not to do, and when to go to a shop

Some of the pages go further than I would. Leoguar's troubleshooting page includes removing a fuse from the battery, putting multimeter probes on the battery terminals and disconnecting brake-sensor wires, and Rad's walks through unplugging cable harnesses at the downtube. I'm leaving those out. Opening a lithium battery or working on its wiring isn't something I'd do or recommend at home. Gazelle says the battery should only be taken apart by experts, and Upway says not to open the display unit, cut wires or work around the system.

Go to a shop or contact the maker's support when:

- the contacts or battery look dirty, damaged or wet. Rad says to stop and not keep riding or charging until support advises you
- the battery won't charge with an intact cable
- the same error code returns after every restart
- the display changed behavior after a crash
- you suspect water reached the motor controller, or there is corrosion
- the battery housing has a gap, which Gazelle says may mean the battery has expanded

Rad also suggests photographing cable routing before you disconnect anything, which is a reminder to leave that to a shop if you aren't sure. If you are shopping, see [what to look for in an electric bike](/what-to-look-for-when-buying-an-electric-bike-or-e-bike/) for warranty and service tips, and [can you ride an e-bike without the battery](/can-you-ride-an-electric-bike-without-the-battery-manually/) if it simply won't power on and you need to get home.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you turn on an electric bike?
Charge the battery, seat it and unlock it if it has a key or switch, press the battery button if there is one, hold the display power button until it lights up, then choose an assist level.

### How do you start an electric bike after it's on?
On pedal assist, turn the pedals and the motor helps. On a throttle bike, use the throttle. Start on the lowest assist level.

### Why won't my electric bike turn on?
Common causes in the pages I read: an uncharged or poorly seated battery, a switch or key not on, a brake lever stuck, a loose display, or a battery in protection mode.

### How long do I hold the power button?
The pages differ: a few seconds (Levy), 2 to 3 seconds or five to be safe (Leoguar). Check your manual.

### Can I fix an e-bike that won't turn on myself?
Do the simple checks: charge, reseat, release the brakes, restart. Leave fuses, wiring and battery repair to a shop.

### Is it safe to turn on a wet e-bike?
Upway says to turn it off, remove the battery if possible and let it dry in a warm, dry place for at least a day first.

### Do I need a key to turn on my e-bike?
Only if the battery has a lock. Many batteries use a key, and some use a button or switch.

## The bottom line

How to turn on a bike with a motor: charge it, seat and unlock the battery, hold the display power button, pick an assist level and pedal. If it won't turn on, check charge, seating, brake levers and the display before anything else, and take it to a shop rather than opening the battery.
