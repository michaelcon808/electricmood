# Where to Get an Electric Scooter Fixed and What It Costs

Where can I get my electric scooter fixed? You have five realistic options: the manufacturer's authorized service center, a local bike shop that works on e-scooters, a specialist e-scooter repair shop, a mobile repair technician who comes to you, or doing simple jobs yourself. If the scooter is still under warranty, start with the manufacturer. On cost, the sellers I read quote anything from about $20 for a small fix to $500 or more for motor or battery work, but those are seller estimates, not a survey of shops.

This page covers where to go, how to find a shop near you, what questions to ask, what repairs cost according to Levy and Gyroor, and how warranty rules interact with independent repair. I haven't repaired or tested any scooter for this post. I can't see where you live either, so I cannot name a shop near you; I show you how to find one.

## Where can I get my electric scooter fixed?

Here are the places, with what Levy and Hiboy (both scooter sellers) say about each. None of these pages name drawbacks for most options, so I have added my own where I say so.

| Option | What the sellers say | My note |
|---|---|---|
| Authorized service center | Technicians trained on your brand; Hiboy adds genuine parts, current diagnostic tools, service records and warranty protection | Not everyone lives near one; Hiboy notes some riders "may not have easy access" |
| Local bike shop | Many now repair e-scooters; quick turnaround, personal service; Hiboy says possibly lower prices | Not every shop does it. Confirm before you bring it in |
| Specialist e-scooter shop | Handles everything from tire changes to electrical faults | May not exist near you |
| Mobile repair | Technician comes to you | Hiboy says major motor repairs, complex electrical faults, frame damage and controller reprogramming may still need a shop |
| DIY | Fine for minor jobs like flat tires and loose parts | Motor, controller and structural work is best left to professionals, per Hiboy and Gyroor |
| Online marketplaces (TaskRabbit, Thumbtack, Fiverr, Handy) | Easy to compare and book; Hiboy suggests a 4.5+ star rating with recent reviews | Quality varies, so vet the reviews |

If the scooter is under warranty, Levy's advice is to contact the manufacturer first, and Hiboy says many manufacturers provide free or discounted repairs for covered issues.

## Where can I get my e scooter fixed near me?

Where can I get my e scooter fixed near me? I can't know where you are, so I cannot name a shop, and a list of shop names from a website I have not checked would be worth little. Here is how to find your own:

- **Check the manufacturer's site** for an authorized service location, or a locator map if it has one. Levy says its own partner map lists certified locations by zip code or city, though Levy's page is promotional.
- **Search Google Maps or Yelp** for "e-scooter repair shop" or "PEV repair" (PEV means personal electric vehicle). Levy says dedicated PEV shops are often the best option for complex repairs in big metro areas.
- **Read the reviews for e-scooter work specifically.** Levy says to pay close attention to reviews that mention electric scooter work, and suggests community forums and Facebook groups too.
- **Phone first.** Ask if they service electric scooters, which brands and how many a month.

If you searched where can I get my electric scooter fixed near me and found only bicycle shops, that is common. Levy lists local bike shops as an option if they service e-scooters, but also warns that a shop that mainly does bicycle work and only occasionally sees e-scooters is a red flag for complex jobs.

## Places that can fix electric scooters near me: what to check

Once you have a few places that can fix electric scooters near me, narrow them down. Levy's checklist, which I think is sensible:

- Do they list e-scooter repair as a core service, not an afterthought?
- Do they work on your brand?
- Are the parts OEM, manufacturer-approved compatible or aftermarket?
- What warranty do they give on parts and labor? Levy says reputable shops typically cover 30 to 90 days.
- Is there a diagnostic fee, and is it applied to the repair?
- What is the turnaround time and are the parts in stock?
- Will they give a written estimate? Levy says: "Always ask for a written estimate before authorizing any repair work."

Hiboy adds: do they test the scooter after the repair, and is pricing hourly or a flat fee?

**Red flags (Levy):** refusing to guarantee repairs, using the cheapest parts without discussing options, replacing parts without diagnosing first, going silent after taking the scooter, and no clear timeline. These are one seller's opinions, but I agree with them.

## Where can I take my electric scooter to get fixed? By problem

Where can I take my electric scooter to get fixed depends on what is wrong. This is my summary of the sellers' advice:

- **Flat tire:** the easiest job to do yourself or have done at a bike shop. See [how to fix a flat tire on an electric scooter](/how-to-fix-a-flat-tire-on-an-electric-scooter/).
- **Brakes:** adjustments and pads are a common DIY job or quick shop visit. See [how to adjust, fix and replace electric scooter brakes](/how-to-fix-adjust-and-change-brake-pads-on-an-electric-scooter/).
- **Wheel:** see [how to remove and replace an electric scooter wheel](/how-to-take-off-and-put-on-an-electric-scooter-back-wheel/).
- **Battery, motor, controller and wiring:** Gyroor says complex issues like motor or battery problems should be handled by professionals, and Hiboy says motor, controller and structural problems are best left to professionals. I agree, and I would not open or rewire a lithium battery pack at home at all.
- **Frame damage:** a professional, per Hiboy.
- **A dead scooter that will not charge:** if it is under warranty, go to the manufacturer first; otherwise a specialist shop can run diagnostics. Levy lists battery voltage, controller error codes, wiring and motor function as a proper diagnostic.

If a battery is swollen, hot or damaged, do not charge it. The [London Fire Brigade](https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/safety/e-bikes-and-e-scooters/how-to-dispose-of-batteries-from-your-e-bike-or-e-scooter/) says damaged or overheated lithium-ion batteries may catch fire and should not go in ordinary bins; ask your local authority where to take it. That is UK guidance, so check your own area. A repair shop can often dispose of or recycle an old battery, but ask.

## How much does it cost to repair an electric scooter?

How much does it cost to repair an electric scooter? The two sellers that publish ranges are Levy and Gyroor. Levy uses "$" and Gyroor's page uses "$" without naming the currency. These are seller estimates and I have not checked them against actual shop invoices, so treat them as a rough guide. I show them side by side and do not average them.

| Repair | Levy | Gyroor |
|---|---|---|
| Battery replacement | $100 to $400, plus $50 to $100 labor | $100 to $500 |
| Tire | New tire $20 to $50; installed $40 to $100 per tire | Puncture tube repair $20 to $50; full replacement $30 to $100 |
| Brakes | $30 to $80, plus $30 to $50 labor | Pads $20 to $80; full system overhaul above $150 |
| Motor | $150 to $500, plus $100 to $150 labor | $200 to $600 |
| Controller or wiring | $50 to $200, plus $50 to $100 labor | $50 to $300 |
| Overall | "From as little as $20 to upwards of $500" | None stated |

Levy's separate shop guide says a diagnostic fee is typically $25 to $50 and is often applied to the repair if you go ahead, and that some shops waive it for simple issues. Gyroor and Hiboy give no labor or diagnostic figures.

What changes the cost, per Levy: the repair type, brand and model, labor rates, complexity (especially electrical), how heavily the scooter is used, disc versus drum brakes, warranty cover and whether you do it yourself.

My arithmetic, as an example: using Levy's numbers, a battery replacement is $150 to $500 once you add labor ($100 to $400 plus $50 to $100), and a professionally fitted tire is $40 to $100. These totals are mine, not Levy's.

## How long does a repair take?

Levy's shop guide gives approximate turnaround times: a flat tire or brake adjustment 30 minutes to 1 hour, a throttle or display replacement 1 to 2 hours, and a battery or motor replacement 2 to 4 hours, though it can be a day or two, or several days, if parts have to be ordered. It says many shops can finish common repairs on popular brands the same day. I have no independent data on turnaround, and waiting for parts is the usual delay.

## Warranty and independent repair

This is where seller advice and the federal guidance differ, so I show both.

- **Seller claim:** Hiboy presents authorized centers as protecting your warranty and shows unauthorized repairs as not offering warranty protection. Levy's cost page says DIY repairs can reduce costs but may void warranties or cause further damage.
- **FTC guidance:** the [FTC's guide to federal warranty law](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law) says a warrantor generally cannot void a warranty just because a consumer used an independent repairer or non-brand parts, but may deny coverage for defects or damage caused by parts or service it did not provide. It also says warrantors may not require customers to use specific brand items or services to keep the warranty in effect, with limited exceptions (for example if the warrantor provides them free).

Those are not the same claim, and I am not a lawyer, so I would not read either as a guarantee. The practical route is to read your warranty terms, keep receipts and photos, and ask the manufacturer in writing what voids cover before an independent shop opens the scooter. The FTC guide is aimed at businesses, and I am relaying its summary, not giving legal advice.

## Repair or replace?

None of the sources gives a rule. Gyroor sets no threshold for choosing between repair and replacement.

My rule of thumb, labelled as opinion: get a written estimate, then compare it with the current price of the same model, which my [scooter cost guide](/how-much-does-an-electric-scooter-cost-to-buy/) helps you check. If the estimate is a large share of that price, or the repair is for the battery on an old scooter whose motor and frame are also worn, replacing may make more sense. If it is a tire, brake pads or a loose part, repair is almost always the cheaper route. ## Before you take it in

A few practical steps, mine and not from a source:

- Photograph the scooter and any damage, and note the serial number.
- Check your warranty dates and keep your receipt.
- Describe the fault precisely: when it happens, any error codes shown on the display.
- Do not charge a battery that is swollen, hot or damaged.
- Get the estimate in writing and agree what happens if the repair costs more.
- Ask what warranty the shop gives on its work.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I get my electric scooter fixed?
An authorized service center if it is under warranty, otherwise a local bike shop that works on e-scooters, a specialist e-scooter repair shop, a mobile repair technician or yourself for simple jobs.

### Where can I get my electric scooter fixed near me?
Check the manufacturer's site for service locations, then search Google Maps or Yelp for "e-scooter repair shop" or "PEV repair" and call first to check they service e-scooters.

### Where can I get my e scooter fixed near me if I only have bike shops?
Call them and ask whether they service electric scooters and your brand. Levy warns that a shop that mostly does bicycles and rarely sees e-scooters is a red flag for complex jobs.

### Are there places that can fix electric scooters near me that come to my home?
Mobile repair technicians exist in some areas, per Levy and Hiboy, but Hiboy says major motor, electrical and frame jobs may still need a shop.

### Where can I take my electric scooter to get fixed under warranty?
Contact the manufacturer first, as Levy advises. Many manufacturers provide free or discounted repairs for covered issues, per Hiboy.

### How much does it cost to repair an electric scooter?
Levy says from as little as $20 to upwards of $500. Gyroor's ranges run from $20 to $600 depending on the part. These are seller estimates.

### How much does it cost to replace an electric scooter battery?
Levy says $100 to $400 for the battery plus $50 to $100 labor; Gyroor says $100 to $500.

### How much does a flat tire repair cost on an electric scooter?
Levy says an installed tire is $40 to $100; Gyroor says $20 to $50 for a puncture tube repair and $30 to $100 for a full replacement.

### Do repair shops charge a diagnostic fee?
Levy says typically $25 to $50, often applied to the repair, and sometimes waived for simple issues.

### Can I repair my electric scooter myself?
Simple jobs like tires and loose bolts, yes. Gyroor and Hiboy say motor, controller, battery and structural problems are best left to professionals.

### Will using an independent shop void my warranty?
The FTC guide says a warrantor generally cannot void a warranty just for using an independent repairer, but may deny cover for damage that service causes. Seller pages say otherwise, so read your terms.

### How long does an electric scooter repair take?
Levy says 30 minutes to an hour for a tire or brake adjustment and 2 to 4 hours for a battery or motor swap, longer if parts must be ordered.

### Is it worth repairing an old electric scooter?
No source gives a threshold. Compare a written estimate with the current price of the same model.
