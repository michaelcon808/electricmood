## Start with how you'll ride

Commuting, trails, cargo or leisure? Your use case decides almost everything else.

## Motor type

### Hub motors

Mounted in the wheel. Affordable and low-maintenance — a great choice for flat commutes.

### Mid-drive motors

Mounted at the cranks. They use the bike's gears, so they climb better and feel more natural.

## Battery capacity

Battery size is measured in watt-hours (Wh = volts × amp-hours). As a rough rule, expect **8–15 Wh per km** depending on assist level, terrain and rider weight.
