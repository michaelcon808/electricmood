# Best Electric Bike Brands 2027

The best electric bike brands 2027 depends on what you value, and the sources point at a short list. For value and range of choice, Lectric, Aventon, Velotric and Ride1Up show up again and again in the tests and rankings I read. For a premium bike with a dealer who can service it, Specialized and Trek are the names the sources use. Be careful with one popular name: Rad Power Bikes, which the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission warned about in November 2025 and which went through bankruptcy.

The most popular electric bike brands 2027 are harder to name than any ranking suggests. I found no public sales ranking by brand. The only hard numbers I found are for the whole direct-to-consumer market, and brand "popularity" in the sources is based on used listings or on the sites' own editorial judgment. I show each measure and what it can't tell you.

I haven't ridden or tested any bike for this page. It draws on Rider Guide, the ebikegearlab site, Electric Bike Report, Flywheel and Upway (both used-bike sellers), PeopleForBikes, Wikipedia and the CPSC. I label what each source tested and what it only claims. Prices and warranties are what those sources listed, not live figures. Checked October 10, 2026.

## How I chose these brands and what the sources measure

Every list measures something different, which explains why they don't match.

- **Rider Guide** (published August 25, 2026; its banner says updated October 7, 2026): 12 brands, built from in-house test data, editorial judgment and brand popularity measured by reader clicks and third-party sales data. No weights are published, and Rider Guide earns affiliate commissions. Some of its claims, such as Lectric "outsells every other e-bike company in America," have no data shown.
- **ebikegearlab** (updated August 26, 2026): six ranked brands plus prose on many more. It doesn't explain its ranking method. It says it earns commissions, and its support and parts claims have no cited evidence. Its Lectric claim of "1 in 10 e-bikes sold in the US" isn't backed by the figure it cites in the body, about 150,000 units shipped in 2025.
- **Electric Bike Report** (July 31, 2026): says it tested over 130 e-bikes in 12 months; I counted brands across its 24 picks. It has affiliate links and sponsored sections.
- **Flywheel** and **Upway:** both sell used e-bikes, so their lists show what is listed for resale, not what sold new. Flywheel's piece has no date and no stated method. Upway's was published November 3, 2025.
- **PeopleForBikes** (published June 24, 2026): the only market-size source, covering the whole direct-to-consumer channel with no brand shares.

I did not find a reliability survey by brand. Which? tests bikes, but its Best Buy names are paywalled and its page had no brand reliability findings. So "best" here means "recommended by named reviewers," not "least likely to break."

## The most popular electric bike brands 2027: what the data shows

Here is what the data does and doesn't say about the most popular electric bike brands 2027 readers ask about.

**Market size.** [PeopleForBikes](https://www.peopleforbikes.org/news/e-bike-market-bigger-than-numbers-show) estimated that about 450,000 direct-to-consumer e-bikes worth about $800 million sold in the U.S. in 2024, plus about 80,000 on peer-to-peer marketplaces, for nearly 1,000,000 e-bikes bought or sold, and e-bikes at about 7% of U.S. bicycle unit sales (against 53% in Germany). It gives no brand market shares. That is the best figure I found, and it covers the market, not brands.

**Used listings.** Flywheel says Rad Power was the most commonly listed e-bike brand on the used market, with about 4.3% of listings, neck and neck with Specialized at about 4.2%, and says Lectric had entered its top 10. Its top 10 together make up 23.8% of the secondary market. Upway's list, a different used-bike seller's, put Specialized first (about 10% of its certified listings), then Aventon, Gazelle, Trek and Rad Power, and said Aventon, Rad Power, Specialized, Gazelle and Lectric are its top five by inventory. Neither list is a sales ranking, and they conflict on order.

**Claims.** Rider Guide's "outsells every other" claim for Lectric and ebikegearlab's "1 in 10" claim are assertions, as noted above.

So when a page calls a brand "the most popular electric bike brand," ask what it measured. The honest answer is that nobody publishes new-bike sales by brand.

## Rad Power Bikes: popular, but read this first

Rad appears on three lists: ranked fourth by ebikegearlab (best for utility and cargo, about $1,200 to $2,200), most commonly listed used brand at Flywheel and fifth at Upway. Here is the other side.

On November 24, 2025, the [CPSC warned consumers](https://cpsc.gov/Warnings/2026/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Immediately-Stop-Using-Batteries-for-E-Bikes-from-Rad-Power-Bikes-Due-to-Fire-Hazard-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death) to stop using three Rad battery models (RP-1304, RAD-S1304Y and HL-RP-S1304). It said it was aware of 31 fire reports, 12 with property damage totaling about $734,500, and that some fires happened while the battery was not charging or the bike was in storage. The CPSC says Rad refused to agree to an acceptable recall, and that Rad said third-party labs retested the batteries and they passed, and that replacing all batteries would put it out of business.

According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rad_Power_Bikes), Rad filed for Chapter 11 on December 15, 2025, with about $32 million in assets and $72 million in liabilities. Life EV Group won the auction with a $13.3 million bid on January 22, 2026, the deal closed March 5, 2026, and the company was renamed Rad Life Mobility. Wikipedia says warranties were not honored for products bought before December 15, 2025, during the bankruptcy, and that the new owner plans a discounted battery replacement program. I didn't verify the later program details. If you own a Rad, check the CPSC warning for your battery model number, which is on a label on the back of the battery.

The lesson for any brand is in the next section.

## How to judge an electric bike brand yourself

Because the rankings don't measure reliability, use these checks. Where I cite a source, it is the source's advice; the rest is my judgment.

1. **Warranty.** Rider Guide's listed warranties: Heybike 2 years (10 years on the frame of its Hero model), Segway 2, Velotric 2 (5 on the frame), Aventon 2 (lifetime frame with registration), Juiced 3 (1 on the battery), Monarc 5, Tenways 2 (5 frame), Lectric 1, Ride1Up 1, Mooncool 1 (3 on the battery). Equiterre, a Quebec environmental nonprofit, suggests a lifetime frame warranty, two years on the motor, and two years or 500 to 800 charge cycles on the battery. See [what to look for in an electric bike](/what-to-look-for-when-buying-an-electric-bike-or-e-bike/).
2. **Company stability.** VanMoof's 2023 bankruptcy (see [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VanMoof)) and Rad's 2025 filing show a warranty is only as good as the company behind it. A bike is also hard to fix if the maker disappears: Equiterre warns that obscure brands may have parts that are hard to find later, and recommends buying from a local shop with a repair shop on site.
3. **Battery certification.** Equiterre says to look for UL, ULC or CSA marks on the battery and charger. The Rad case shows why. See the injury and fire section of my [electric bike pros and cons](/electric-bike-advantages-and-disadvantages-pros-cons/).
4. **Standard parts.** Equiterre names Shimano, SRAM and KMC as drivetrain brands to look for, and Bosch, Shimano, Yamaha, Panasonic and Brose as battery brands.
5. **Price.** Brand names change the price a lot; see [how much an electric bike costs](/how-much-do-electric-bikes-cost-why-so-expensive-and-most-expensive/) and [best budget electric bikes](/best-budget-electric-bikes/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best electric bike brands 2027?
The sources point to Lectric, Aventon, Velotric and Ride1Up for value, and Specialized and Trek for premium bikes with dealer service.

### What are the top electric bike brands 2027?
Across three rankings, Lectric, Aventon and Velotric appear most often. Specialized and Trek lead the used-bike lists.

### What are the most popular electric bike brands 2027?
No source publishes new-bike sales by brand. Used-listing lists put Rad Power, Specialized, Aventon and Lectric near the top.

### Is Lectric a good brand?
Rider Guide and ebikegearlab both rank it first for value. Its warranty is one year per Rider Guide.

### Is Rad Power Bikes safe to buy?
The CPSC warned in November 2025 about three Rad battery models and 31 fire reports, and Rad filed for bankruptcy in December 2025. The company now operates as Rad Life Mobility.

### Which e-bike brands have the best warranties?
Rider Guide lists Monarc at 5 years and Heybike's Hero model at 10 years on the frame, while Lectric and Ride1Up are 1 year.

### Are expensive e-bike brands more reliable?
I found no reliability survey that answers it. Premium brands offer dealer networks and service.

### Are any e-bike brands made in the USA?
Most are assembled or built abroad, and sources conflict on several. See my e-bikes made in the USA guide.

## The bottom line

The best electric bike brands 2027 depends on value or service: Lectric, Aventon, Velotric and Ride1Up for value, Specialized and Trek for dealer-backed premium bikes. The most popular electric bike brands can't be named from public sales data, and the top electric bike brands lists mostly rest on editorial judgment, so check the warranty and the company's stability before you pay.
